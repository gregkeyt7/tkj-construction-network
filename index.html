Insurance-Related Construction Support

Storm damage documentation and repair estimates without the confusion.

We help document visible damage, organize photos, provide repair estimates, and help homeowners understand the construction side of storm-related roof and property damage.

We do not act as public adjusters or insurance representatives. We provide construction inspections, repair estimates, and documentation support.

Request Storm Damage Review

Storm Damage Checklist

  • Take wide photos of the property
  • Photograph visible roof or exterior concerns
  • Save interior leak photos if water entered
  • Avoid unsafe roof climbing
  • Request a construction-side inspection

Project Checklist

Before requesting your estimate

  • Know the project type or problem area
  • Take photos from safe locations
  • Write down leaks, dates, and storm details
  • Measure rooms or estimate square footage if possible
  • Share your budget range and urgency
